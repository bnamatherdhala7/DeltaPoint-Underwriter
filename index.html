Common questions

Does this do a hard credit pull?
No. We use read-only access via a soft pull only. Connecting to DeltaPoint never affects your credit score.
How does Direct Pay work?
When your loan is approved, Direct Pay routes proceeds directly to selected creditors. You authorize each creditor in-app and DeltaPoint sends payment at loan funding.
How accurate are the rate simulations?
Simulations use live lender rate tiers and your real credit data. Median model deviation is under 0.4% APR versus final underwriting.
Is my financial data safe?
Yes — AES-256 encryption, SOC 2 Type II certified, zero data selling. Revoke access any time from within the app.
